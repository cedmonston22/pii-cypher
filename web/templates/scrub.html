Scrub a batch

Upload Schedule K-1s. Identifiers are replaced with tokens and the originals are encrypted under a key belonging to this batch.

Up to 25 files, 10 MB each, 50 MB per batch, 20 pages per document. Processing is synchronous, so the batch is capped to keep the request responsive.

What is replaced

TypeFields on the K-1
EINA — partnership employer identification number
SSN / TINE — partner's SSN or TIN
AddressB and F — street, city, state and ZIP

Names are deliberately out of policy and are left on the form.
